G.O.T Games Support

How can we help?

Search common problems or choose a topic below. These guides cover the most common things you can fix yourself.

Account & sign-in

I can't sign in
  1. Check that you are using the same sign-in method and email you used when you registered.
  2. Refresh the page and try again. If the session expired, sign in again.
  3. If the password is the problem, use the account provider's password-reset option.
  4. Make sure cookies and site data are not being blocked for this site.
My profile changes are not showing
  1. Open your profile and check whether you are signed into the correct account.
  2. Save the changes and wait for the success message before leaving the page.
  3. Refresh the profile. If it still looks old, close and reopen the page.

Anime, seasons & episodes

An anime is missing from search
  1. Try the official or alternate title, and search using fewer words.
  2. Check spelling and remove punctuation or season labels from the search.
  3. If it still does not appear, the external anime catalogue may not have a matching entry yet.
The season or part number is wrong
  1. Check the anime's actual season/part naming. A story part is not always the same thing as a streaming season.
  2. For playlist review items, check the video title and thumbnail before approving the detected season.
  3. If you are an admin, correct the season in the review queue rather than approving a guess.
An episode or reaction link is missing
  1. Check the anime title, season and episode range on the Anime page.
  2. Some links may be waiting for admin review before they appear publicly.
  3. For a missing or incorrect link, report the anime, season, episode number and what you expected to see.
Playlist review says “Anime details needed”
  1. Open the review item and check the detected anime title and video title.
  2. Confirm the AniList match is the correct series or stage, not just a similar name.
  3. Check the season/part and episode number against the video title and thumbnail.
  4. Only approve after the details match. If no exact match exists, leave it for manual review.

Watch history

My watched episode count looks wrong
  1. Confirm the correct anime, season and episode range are recorded.
  2. Check whether the episode is verified or still waiting for review.
  3. Refresh the page after the record is updated. The verified count should reflect verified episode records, not just a video being listed.
A watch-history entry has missing anime details
  1. Check that the entry has the correct anime title and AniList identity.
  2. Confirm the season and episode range are valid.
  3. Ask an admin to repair the record if the identity cannot be matched confidently. Do not guess an AniList ID.

Website & loading

A page is blank or stuck loading
  1. Refresh the page once and check your internet connection.
  2. Close duplicate tabs, then reopen the page.
  3. Try a private/incognito tab to check whether a cached page or browser extension is interfering.
  4. If one page fails while others work, report which page and what you clicked.
Updates are not appearing
  1. Refresh the page. On a browser, a hard refresh may clear an old cached copy.
  2. Close the tab completely and reopen the site.
  3. Wait a little if a site update was just published; deployment and browser caching can take time.
Notifications are not appearing
  1. Open the Notifications page and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Refresh the page and check again.
  3. Browser push notifications, if supported, also require permission in your browser/device settings. In-site notifications do not necessarily use push alerts.
No help articles match that search. Try a different phrase.

Still stuck?

Can’t find the answer? Send a report directly to the G.O.T. site owner. Include the page, what you were trying to do, and what happened. For anime issues, add the anime, season, and episode if relevant. Never include your password or private account credentials.
